Recall notice · September 17, 2026

CO2 Trust is not active right now.

We have paused the business while we investigate a supplier’s report that certain biochar we previously sold may contain elevated arsenic. Distribution has stopped. This page is the public site while that work continues.

Email recall@co2trust.earth Official statement

Where things stand

The company, the product, and this website

Distribution has stopped

As soon as the supplier reported higher-than-anticipated arsenic levels, CO2T stopped distributing biochar from that source.

The review is still open

We are analyzing certain biochar we sold or supplied. This notice is a precaution while that work continues.

The rest of the site is off

The marketplace, checkout, accounts, and partner tools are unavailable. An old link on this domain brings you here.

If you have CO2T biochar

What to do with biochar you already have

The steps below follow the customer letter. The FAQ explains the 70 ppm biochar result, the 5% to 10% mix, and using a bed again.

Still on its own

Biochar you can still separate

If it has not been mixed into soil, fertilizer, or other growing media, and you can still separate it, set it aside and keep it there. Do not use, sell, transfer, or dispose of that biochar.

Already in the ground

Biochar mixed into soil or media

Do not dig it out or try to separate it. Leave that soil or media where it is. Out of caution, do not eat crops grown with this biochar as a soil amendment.

You still need the space

The bed, pot, or field has to be used again

The letter asks you not to dig mixed-in biochar out, and not to eat crops grown with it. The FAQ explains why 70 ppm in the biochar is not 70 ppm in the soil, and what to do if you used more than 5% to 10%.

Passed along

Biochar no longer with you

If you supplied that biochar, or biochar-grown products, to someone else, send us that person or business’s name and how to reach them.

Send questions to the recall desk

CO2T will share more information as the investigation produces results. We intend to cooperate with Oregon regulatory and administrative agencies if they ask. The customer letter is the full statement.
